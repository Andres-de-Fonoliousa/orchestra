## Post Body

AI development tools often suffer from "chat amnesia." Every fresh session requires re-explaining project context, API conventions, and recent architectural decisions. It's a massive productivity killer.

I built **Orchestra** to fix this.

Orchestra implements a structured memory bridge that syncs across every chat you start, ensuring your AI behaves like a consistent, long-term team member.

### What Orchestra provides:
- **Contextual Continuity:** New chat sessions automatically inherit your project's identity, knowledge, and active handoff state.
- **Git-Backed Immutable Brain:** Your memory is a verifiable Git repository. You can query, search, and audit everything the AI has ever learned about your project.
- **Autonomous Agent Swarms:** Orchestra orchestrates complex hierarchies of specialized agents (Backend, Frontend, Security, DevOps, Tester) to deliver production-grade features.

### Why use it?
It's built for developers who want to move beyond single-prompt engineering. By formalizing your workflow and memory, Orchestra lets you build complex, production-ready systems without the manual overhead.
